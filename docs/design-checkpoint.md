# Mitos phone design checkpoint

Direction approved in this session: section 11 of the brief, including its five
categories and contexts. The first prototype is a ledger of the household's
actual captured work. Item was implemented before Everything.

Tokens: paper #f7f7f5, ink #141414, secondary ink #686864, separator #dcdcd6,
selection/link/capture blue #1854df, overdue-only red #b52727. Dark mode follows
the system and inverts paper/ink. Geist carries prose; Geist Mono carries dates,
counts and category marks. Phone base size is 17 px in rem units.

Everything: title and count, two rows of text filters, then a full-bleed ruled
list. The title remains one line; only meaningful metadata creates a second.
Item: large editable title, optional labeled sections, the permanent log, links,
then a quiet settings disclosure. Empty sections are revealed by Add details.

No dashboard, decorative cards, illustrations, category background colors or
automatic animation. A 680 px maximum bounds the phone layout on wider screens;
no desktop rail or master-detail treatment is chosen before review.

The Ready/Due/Review navigation positions and Shape/Work on this actions are
visible but explicitly disabled. Those screens and integrations are not built
through this checkpoint. Settings is also deferred until design approval.
