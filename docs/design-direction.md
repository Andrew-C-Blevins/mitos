# Mitos visual direction

Andrew's 2026-09-21 feedback supersedes the aesthetic restrictions in section 11
of the original brief: the stark black-and-white ledger feels dull and too close
to Mise. Keep the information architecture and useful density, but explore a
distinct personality for Mitos.

Andrew requested three contrasting concepts before committing to a redesign:

- Studio: expressive plum/lilac and golden accents, crisp sans-serif type,
  distinct rounded surfaces and a prominent next step.
- Garden: soft green grounds, forest accents, gentle serif headings, open list
  spacing and a quiet document surface.
- Fieldnotes: blue ink, ochre and paper tones, book-like typography, square
  controls and a collected notebook feel.

The comparison uses the same representative items in phone and desktop views.
Concept details are illustrative and do not change any item records. Select and
refine a direction before applying it to the app. A good next review compares
Everything, a populated Item and capture, including dark mode and readable small
screens. Design review is not approval for later feature milestones.
